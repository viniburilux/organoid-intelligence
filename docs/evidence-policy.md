# Evidence policy

This repository is an open research record, not a claim amplifier. Every public statement should make clear whether it is a reported fact, a result of a bounded analysis, an inference, a hypothesis or an opportunity for future work.

## Claim labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Reported fact** | Directly supported by a primary paper, official archive or source repository. |
| **Observed result** | Produced by a declared computational analysis with a defined dataset, task and protocol. |
| **Qualified inference** | A reasonable interpretation of observed results that remains narrower than a causal or biological claim. |
| **Hypothesis** | A proposition retained because it can motivate a falsifiable next test. |
| **Opportunity** | A possible future direction that has not been established by the current record. |
| **Blocked** | A question that cannot be tested cleanly with the available data or access boundary. |

## Evidence hierarchy

Primary papers, official dataset records, official documentation and source repositories take priority over summaries, model outputs, social posts and inherited project descriptions. A source link is necessary but not sufficient: task, scope, license, version, access and limitations must also be understood before a claim is promoted.

## Reproducibility boundary

The public edition reports protocols and aggregate outcomes where they can be explained without publishing restricted artifacts. It does not promise that every public finding can be reproduced from this repository alone. Some results depend on source artifacts, private laboratory workspaces, raw data or internal execution history that are intentionally not distributed here.

## Privacy boundary

The following remain private by design:

- operational ASIE state and frontier decisions;
- internal hypotheses, cycle logs and resource budgets;
- raw or restricted biological data and acquisition credentials;
- unpublished experiment machinery and intermediate artifacts;
- private prompts, orchestration details and laboratory-specific automation;
- any artifact that would allow reconstruction of the private research environment.

The public record may describe the existence and conceptual role of these layers, but it does not publish their operational contents.

## Scientific boundary

Synthetic data and synthetic control can validate software interfaces, metrics and experimental protocols. They cannot, on their own, establish biological learning, memory, intelligence, consciousness or equivalence to organoid tissue.

## Update rule

A public finding should be updated only when a new source, analysis or contradiction changes its status. The update should preserve the previous wording when it explains why the interpretation changed. The project should prefer a visible correction over a silent rewrite.
