# Contributing

Contributions are welcome when they improve clarity, provenance, reproducibility or the public understanding of the evidence.

Please keep contributions scoped to the public research record. A pull request should explain which page or claim it changes, identify the source or result behind the change and state whether the wording is a reported fact, observed result, qualified inference, hypothesis or opportunity.

Do not contribute operational ASIE state, private frontier decisions, raw or restricted data, credentials, unpublished laboratory machinery, private prompts or third-party material without verified redistribution rights. If a contribution would expose the private laboratory, do not open a public pull request; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

When a new result changes an existing interpretation, preserve the previous wording or explain the change in the relevant timeline and research map. Visible correction is preferred to silent revision.
